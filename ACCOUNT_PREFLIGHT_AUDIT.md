# Development executable-account preflight (updated)

The candidate corporate-action screen is **nonblocking**. The verified NSE Clearing settlement-business-day artifact now exists, and the chronology can start. A missing dividend payment date creates an unspendable receivable; a missing bonus-credit date creates an unavailable economic share claim. Neither is a reason to reject the entire account before testing whether it actually owns the security. The preflight `corporate_action_preflight_pass` field means only that the candidate screen permits a chronological ledger, **not** that every future held event is resolved.

The corrected run found the potential DHANI rights event to be **actually held by both accounts** on 2018-02-09. The partial account paths therefore stop at that event; see [`ACCOUNT_DEVELOPMENT_AUDIT.md`](ACCOUNT_DEVELOPMENT_AUDIT.md). The former blanket preflight statement that accounts could not even start was too broad. This change was made before any complete account or holdout performance was inspected.

The refreshed [`preflight_summary.csv`](results/account_preflight/preflight_summary.csv) counts 4,736 selected security-months, 548 corporate-action records in normal holding/exit windows, 493 economic dividend candidates without payment dates, 23 bonus candidates without credit dates, and one candidate DHANI right. The 44 unresolved actions after a security's first selection are an overinclusive contingency, not proof of a held blocker. [`candidate_corporate_actions.csv`](results/account_preflight/candidate_corporate_actions.csv) retains each candidate and source status. Input SHA-256 hashes are in [`input_manifest.csv`](results/account_preflight/input_manifest.csv).

The DHANI accepted research-signal right uses theoretical ex-rights value, which cannot be substituted for executable-account sale proceeds or a verified account claim value. The ledger records the actual held entitlement separately and stops the NAV path at exposure, without treating it as cash. No post-2023-03-31 market value or holdout output was inspected.
