- [UPL May 2019 bonus announcement filed with NSE](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/UPL_17052019145757_UPLAFR31032019_173.pdf): one fully paid bonus share per two existing fully paid shares; fractional shares are not issued as ordinary equity.
- [UPL June 2019 EGM notice and resolution](https://www.upl-ltd.com/policy_compliances/lW3QoLwVnxOitr5KTa3Ke7TXJCVvupf4yASex4hi/UPL_Limited_EGM_Notice_21-06-2019.pdf): whole bonus shares are credited to members, rank pari passu, and participate in subsequent corporate actions. The Board may ignore or arrange for fractional interests for members' benefit.

The same fact appears in the [issuer's later annual report filed with SGX](https://links.sgx.com/1.0.0/corporate-announcements/AOKC7YE85Q8HI81V/675000_Singapore-Notice_and_Annual_Report_2020_21_UPL.pdf).

This later document is used only to classify the 2019 interest, never as a price, trading signal, or holdout-performance observation.
- [UPL 2024 rights terms](https://www.upl-ltd.com/downloads/right-issues-pdf/FinalRICommitteeOutcome.pdf): one right per eight fully paid ordinary shares held on 26 November 2024.
