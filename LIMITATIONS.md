# Limitations and scope

- **Unpriced fractional claim.** VM and FIXVOL retain separate 0.5-unit UPL 2019 bonus-sale-proceeds claims. Their cash amount and payment date were not established, so no proceeds were invented and the claims are excluded from NAV. This creates a small, unquantified conservative valuation limitation; the claims do not confer 2024 rights.
- **Received securities.** A mandatory unlisted received security is carried explicitly without a fabricated ex-date or prelisting price. VLEGOV recognition begins with the accepted first observable official market quote. A period of zero marked value may understate economic wealth before listing.
- **Corporate-action research returns.** Four development bonus-debenture strict daily total returns remain unresolved. Their monthly research-feature treatment is narrower and does not repair daily shadow risk. The two Britannia events excluded eight specific momentum formations. The final accounts disclose passive rights/claim conventions rather than treating theoretical entitlement values as sale proceeds.
- **Paper adaptation.** The tested portfolio is long winners plus cash, without leverage or a short loser leg. Results cannot be read as a replication of the paper's long-short crash or return claims. The 12% target is an exposure formula, not a guaranteed realised-volatility or drawdown ceiling.
- **Execution model.** Daily open/close/volume data cannot establish intraday order-book depth or a guaranteed opening-auction fill. The 1% lagged-ADV cap and square-root impact curve are declared approximations; real impact and partial fills could differ. Missing quotes and ADV are never replaced with fabricated trades or capacity.
- **Settlement and tax.** Uniform conservative T+2 settlement-business-day availability and the per-account FIFO/capital-gains tax convention simplify actual historical settlement and investor-specific taxation. Cash earns 0%; the published TRI is untaxed and frictionless, so benchmark-relative growth is descriptive rather than after-tax alpha.
- **Inference.** The holdout has 742 sessions but only 36 monthly decisions. Drawdowns and tail observations are path-dependent; the fixed-control comparison is informative but does not establish a causal volatility-timing benefit. VM had lower holdout volatility/drawdown, yet lower CAGR and Sharpe than the fixed controls.
- **Source boundary.** Large raw NSE archives and one-off reconstruction programs are omitted. The accepted processed panels and compact runtime inputs are shipped, so the documented command reproduces the full signal, account, metric, and figure path from that audited boundary. Public-source URLs and hashes remain in `data/raw/OMITTED_SOURCE_MANIFEST.csv`.
